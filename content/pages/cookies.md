---
title: Cookie policy
---

Our website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't track your visit.

**Map.** The map on our Visit section is provided by Google Maps. It only loads if you press **Show map**. When it loads, Google may set cookies and see your IP address, under Google's own privacy policy.

**Fonts.** Our page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. No cookies are set.

If we add anything else that uses cookies in future, such as visitor statistics, we'll update this page and ask for your permission first.
