---
title: Allergen information
---

Our food is prepared in a small kitchen where all 14 major allergens are handled, including milk, eggs, wheat (gluten), nuts, peanuts, sesame, soya, celery, mustard, fish, crustaceans, molluscs, lupin and sulphites.

**If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please tell us before you order.** We'll tell you exactly what's in each item.

Please also remember that our café is home to cats. If you're allergic to cats, a visit may not be right for you.
