## Visits

Visits last 1 hour. We take up to 15 guests at a time, including children, and no more than 4 children at once.

## Children

Children must be aged 5 or over, and supervised by an adult at all times.

## Payment

Please pay when you arrive, by cash or card. Any payments made are non-refundable.

## House rules

- Let sleeping cats lie, and let the cats come to you.
- Please sanitise your hands when you arrive.
- Please don't feed the cats human food.

These rules are there to keep our cats happy and everyone safe. We may ask anyone who doesn't follow them to leave.
