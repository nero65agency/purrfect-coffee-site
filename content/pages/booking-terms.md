## Visits

Visits last 1 hour. We take up to 12 guests at a time, including children, and no more than 4 children at once.

## Children

Children must be aged 5 or over, and supervised by an adult at all times.

## Payment

Depending on the day, you'll either pay online when you book (by card, through SumUp) or when you arrive (by cash or card). The booking page tells you which. If you pay online, your places are held for 15 minutes while you pay. All payments are non-refundable.

## House rules

- Let sleeping cats lie, and let the cats come to you.
- Please sanitise your hands when you arrive.
- Please don't feed the cats human food.
- Everyone visiting is asked to agree to our disclaimer.

These rules are there to keep our cats happy and everyone safe. We may ask anyone who doesn't follow them to leave.
